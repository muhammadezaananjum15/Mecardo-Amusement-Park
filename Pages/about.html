Our Story

Mercado Amusement Park stands as a testament to vision, innovation, and the relentless pursuit of excellence. Developed by Seth Groups of Companies, one of the nation's most respected conglomerates, this 45-acre wonderland took five years of meticulous planning and construction to become reality.

What began as a dream to create a world-class entertainment destination has evolved into the nation's pride. Our park features six uniquely themed zones, over 50 attractions, and countless dining and entertainment options that cater to visitors of all ages.

Since our grand opening in 2020, we have welcomed over 5 million visitors from across the country and around the world. Our commitment to safety, innovation, and guest satisfaction has earned us numerous industry awards and, most importantly, the trust of families who return year after year.
